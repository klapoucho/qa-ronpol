**Run:** 2026-11-26-issue-14-marketplace-buyer-buys-an-offer · **Reviewer session:** `-s=2026-11-26-issue-14-review` (profile `scratch/review/profile`) · **Date:** 2026-11-26
**Env:** http://localhost:3000 · Rolnopol 1.126.0 (Docker) · Google Chrome 158.0.8127.88 via playwright-cli 0.1.24, 1366x768 (R8 at 375x812) · API: Node fetch with `X-Synthetic-Token`
**Accounts:** fresh reviewer users from `scratch/review/seed.mjs`: seller S, buyer B, buyer C, rival RV, and an extra user for R11. Tag `rvw9550`. No demo accounts. Nothing was deleted, stopped or reset.

## 1. Verdict: GAPS CLOSED

The tester's run is thorough. Every acceptance criterion has a UI check with an API read-back and a negative control, and the known bugs were re-checked. Five gaps were open: the Financial page was never opened (AC-5 "financial history"), the own-offer gate and the logged-out gate were not tried in the UI, the console was not saved on a refusal, and ownership was not read back in the UI. I closed all five. My experiments found two new minor problems: Browse after browser Back, and the Create Offer list after a buy. They also show that the known bug about balance decimals is mis-scoped. The tester's FAIL verdict stands: four known major bugs are reachable from the buyer's path.

## 4. New problems

### N1. After browser Back, Browse ignores the filter shown in the filter box — minor
- **Steps:** 1. As buyer B, open Marketplace → Browse and type `rvw9550` in "Filter offers by any text...". Only the matching offer shows (1711). 2. Open the Financial page from the top navigation. 3. Press the browser's Back button.
- **Expected:** Browse shows the offers matching the text in the filter box, or the box is empty.
- **Actual:** the filter box still reads `rvw9550`, but Browse lists 9 unfiltered offers (demo offers 1, 3, 4, 6, 12, 13, 14, 16 and 17 from Demo User and John Brown) that do not contain the text. Typing any character in the box applies the filter again. A buyer coming back after checking a purchase sees offers that do not match the search they think is active. 2 of 2.
- **Evidence:** `artifacts/review/R6-back-from-financial.png`, `artifacts/review/R3-R6-direct-url-and-back.json` (`R6`), `artifacts/review/R6b-back-repeat.json` (`beforeNav` [1711], `afterBack` 9 other ids, `afterRetype` [1711])
- **Env:** http://localhost:3000/marketplace.html · Chrome 158.0.8127.88 via playwright-cli 0.1.24, 1366x768 · Rolnopol 1.126.0 · fresh buyer
- **Hint (optional):** the browser restores the input's value, but the list is rendered from the empty in-memory filter.

### N2. A field bought a moment ago cannot be listed for resale until the page is reloaded — minor
- **Steps:** 1. As buyer B, buy S's field offer 1711 (field 1826) in Browse → Buy Now → Buy Now. 2. Without reloading, open Create Offer and choose Item Type "field" (or switch the type away and back).
- **Expected:** the field just bought is in the item list. It belongs to the buyer (the API's `/fields` returns it).
- **Actual:** the list shows the buyer's earlier fields (1824, 1825, 1828, 1829) but not 1826. After a reload, 1826 is listed. The buyer who wants to resell is told, in effect, that they do not own what they just paid for.
- **Evidence:** `artifacts/review/R10-create-offer-dropdown-after-buy.json` (`optsAfter` without 1826, `optsAfterReload` with it, `fieldInApi: true`), `artifacts/review/R10-create-offer-after-buy-no-reload.png`
- **Env:** as N1 · 1 of 1. The cause is the one the tester read in `marketplace.js`: after a buy only the balance, offers, my-offers and transactions are reloaded, not fields or animals.

### N3. Correction to finding 13: Marketplace prices also lack fixed decimals, and each screen formats amounts its own way — cosmetic
- **Steps:** list a field at 20.20 and open Browse. Buy it, then open Transaction History and the Financial page.
- **Expected:** one money format across the product, for example "20.20 ROL" everywhere.
- **Actual:** the Browse card shows "20.2 ROL" and the Marketplace history "-20.2 ROL". The balance shows "10.1 ROL". The Financial page shows "+20,20 ROL" / "18,53 ROL" (Polish decimal comma, two decimals). The Marketplace header shows "508 163,38". Finding 13 and `.qa/bugs/ghissue-14/ui-marketplace-browse--balance-without-fixed-decimals.md` say that prices "always have two" decimals. That is true only for prices whose second decimal is not 0. The bug's title, expected value and regression check should cover prices and history amounts too.
- **Evidence:** `artifacts/review/R4a-back-to-back-end.png` (card "20.2 ROL" next to "31.31 ROL"), `artifacts/review/R4b-two-offers-exceed-balance.json` (history "-20.2 ROL", balance "10.1 ROL"), `artifacts/review/R1-financial-page-buyer-and-seller.json` (Financial "+20,20 ROL")
- **Env:** as N1
- **Note:** the Financial page rows read only "Marketplace purchase" / "Marketplace sale", with no item or counterparty. Recorded as an observation, not a finding.
